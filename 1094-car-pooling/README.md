<h2><a href="https://leetcode.com/problems/car-pooling/">1094. Car Pooling</a></h2><h3>Medium</h3><hr>

A car with a fixed seat `capacity` drives only east. Each trip `[numPassengers, from, to]` picks people up at `from` and drops them at `to`. Decide whether every trip can be served without ever exceeding the capacity.

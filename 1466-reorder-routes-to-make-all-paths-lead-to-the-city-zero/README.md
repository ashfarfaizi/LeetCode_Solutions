<h2><a href="https://leetcode.com/problems/reorder-routes-to-make-all-paths-lead-to-the-city-zero/">1466. Reorder Routes to Make All Paths Lead to the City Zero</a></h2><h3>Medium</h3><hr>

There are `n` cities joined by `n - 1` one-way roads that form a tree when directions are ignored. Find the minimum number of roads whose direction must be reversed so that every city can reach city `0`.

<h2><a href="https://leetcode.com/problems/online-election/">911. Online Election</a></h2><h3>Medium</h3><hr>

Votes arrive at strictly increasing times, each for some person. Build a structure that answers queries "who was leading at time t?", where the leader is whoever has the most votes so far and a tie is won by the person who received the most recent vote among those tied.

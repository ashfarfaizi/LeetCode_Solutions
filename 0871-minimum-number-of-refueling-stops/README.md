<h2><a href="https://leetcode.com/problems/minimum-number-of-refueling-stops/">871. Minimum Number of Refueling Stops</a></h2><h3>Hard</h3><hr>

A car with a limited starting fuel drives toward a target, passing gas stations at known distances that each hold a fixed amount of fuel. Find the fewest refuelling stops needed to reach the target, or report that it can't be done.

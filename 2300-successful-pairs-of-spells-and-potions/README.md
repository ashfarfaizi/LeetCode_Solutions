<h2><a href="https://leetcode.com/problems/successful-pairs-of-spells-and-potions/">2300. Successful Pairs of Spells and Potions</a></h2><h3>Medium</h3><hr>

Each spell and each potion has a strength, and a spell–potion pair is successful when the product of their strengths is at least `success`. For every spell, return how many potions would form a successful pair with it.

<h2><a href="https://leetcode.com/problems/dota2-senate/">649. Dota2 Senate</a></h2><h3>Medium</h3><hr>

Senators from two parties, Radiant and Dire, act in turn order across repeated rounds. On their turn a senator may remove the voting right of an opponent or, once only their party remains, declare victory. Assuming everyone plays optimally, return which party wins.

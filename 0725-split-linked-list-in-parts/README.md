<h2><a href="https://leetcode.com/problems/split-linked-list-in-parts/">725. Split Linked List in Parts</a></h2><h3>Medium</h3><hr>

Split a singly linked list into `k` consecutive parts whose lengths differ by at most one, with earlier parts never shorter than later ones. Parts may be empty (`null`) when the list has fewer than `k` nodes.
